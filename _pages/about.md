Welcome to My Website!
======

My research focused on Financial History. 

My PhD examines Hong Kong’s financial history between 1866 and 2010. My thesis comprehensively reconstructs the long-run financial ecosystem of Hong Kong by examining its financial institutions, investors, and connections with international capital markets. It shows that Hong Kong’s colonial financial system was shaped not only by international banks and colonial institutions, but also by local financial networks, alongside the flows of capital and information that connected the colony to overseas markets.

My PhD is funded by [Centre for Economics, Policy and History (CEPH)](https://ceph.ie/), an economic history lab collaboration between Queen's University Belfast and Trinity College Dublin. My supervisors are [Prof. John Turner](https://pure.qub.ac.uk/en/persons/john-turner/) and [Dr. Chris Colvin](https://pure.qub.ac.uk/en/persons/chris-colvin/).

Before I came to Belfast, I completed BSc in Economics at University College London and MSc Economic History (Research) at London School of Economics.
