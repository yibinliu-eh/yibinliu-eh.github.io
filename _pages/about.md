Welcome to My Website!
======
My research focused on Financial History. 
First Year Project: Hong Kong Banking Crises since 1865: Identification, Causes and Consequences
Second Year Project: Who are investing in Hong Kong: An analysis on HSBC shareholders
Third Year Project: Information and the Empire: HSBC shares on the London Stock Exchange, 1866–1898
My PhD is funded by Centre for Economics, Policy and History (CEPH), an economic history lab collaboration between
Queen’s University Belfast and Trinity College Dublin.
